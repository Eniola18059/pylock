Rules

  1. The plan is locked. The only allowed change is at the Week 8 checkpoint.
  2. Two commits a day. No commits means the day didn’t count.
  3. Friday demo. Show the week’s output to someone, even as a screen recording.
  4. Scrape responsibly. Public business information only, respect robots.txt and each site’s terms, slow requests down, and don’t hoard or message people’s personal data.
  5. Floor of 25 Python hours a week. A short week stays short. Don’t stack the missing hours onto weekends.

The blunt part

Forty hours of Python on top of client delivery and pastoral work won’t happen every week. Decide now how much of the 40 is Python and how much is client work, and protect the floor.

Automation and scraping now run through weeks 2 to 6 and 9 to 15, so those weeks are denser. If you fall behind, cut in this order: Playwright, Telegram alerts, Excel export. Don’t cut tests or deployment.

Payment gateways and PHP/Laravel are left out on purpose. They belong in month 5 and later, once this stack is solid.
